How BlockFund works

A token, an exchange and a website, all small enough to read in an afternoon.

Technology

The stack behind the site

BF is a fixed-supply ERC-20 deployed on Base Sepolia, the public testnet of Base, an Ethereum layer-2. We moved off Ethereum Sepolia after its faucets started requiring a mainnet balance and the network neared end-of-life.

  • BlockFund.sol: the BF token. One million tokens, minted once.
  • BlockFundSwap.sol: our fixed-rate exchange plus a daily BF faucet.
  • This website: plain HTML/CSS/JS on an Express backend, reading the chain directly over JSON-RPC.

How to get BF

Faucet, swap, done

Because BF lives on a testnet it has no real value, and neither does the ETH you pay with. The whole process is free:

  • Claim free Base Sepolia ETH from a faucet.
  • Swap it for BF on BlockFund Swap. WETH and test USDC work too.
  • Or skip the swap: claim the free daily BF drip right from the swap page.
