Use

Wallet to BF in five steps

Most steps are a single click. Have a browser wallet like MetaMask, Rabby or Coinbase Wallet ready.

  1. Install a wallet

    Any EVM browser wallet works. Write down your recovery phrase and never share it. We will never ask for it.

    Get MetaMask

  2. Add Base Sepolia

    Adds the network (chain ID 84532) to your wallet and switches to it.

  3. Claim free test ETH

    Paste your address into one of the faucets below. A few cents' worth of test ETH covers dozens of transactions.

    See faucets

  4. Swap for BF

    Connect on the swap page, enter an amount and confirm. Or claim the free daily BF drip.

    Open swap

  5. Show BF in your wallet

    Wallets hide unknown tokens until you import them. One click adds BF to your asset list.

Faucets

Free Base Sepolia ETH

Ethereum Sepolia faucets now require a mainnet balance. These Base Sepolia faucets don't. Limits change often, so try another if one is dry.
